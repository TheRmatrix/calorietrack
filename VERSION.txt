CalorieTrack 1.2

Changes:
- Multi-source food lookup: Open Food Facts + USDA FoodData Central fallback.
- Barcode lookup tries CalorieTrack local cache, Open Food Facts regional/global sources, then USDA branded/reference data.
- Search combines Open Food Facts and USDA results and removes duplicates.
- Barcode normalization handles common EAN/UPC leading-zero variants.
- Home weight card now refreshes independently from the dedicated weight history.
- Legacy daily weights are migrated into the dedicated history and persisted.
- Existing localStorage key retained for compatibility with CalorieTrack 1.0/1.1 data.
- No Apple Health/steps integration added.
