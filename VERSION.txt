CalorieTrack 1.1

Multi-source food lookup upgrade.

Food lookup now:
- Checks CalorieTrack's local barcode memory first.
- Uses Open Food Facts universal barcode lookup with barcode normalization candidates.
- Falls back across regional Open Food Facts endpoints for Chile, South America and Europe.
- Uses global + regional Open Food Facts searches.
- Lets users save a missing barcode product manually for future scans.
- Keeps the existing CalorieTrack UI and local-first storage.

No Apple Health / steps integration is included in this release.
